# P8: Lugh on Dannan and Dagda

The plan for Lugh's move onto the stack (stack document, `../dagda/TODO/2026-11-03-stack.md`, "What changes for Lugh"),
against Dagda's slices as the maintainer ruled them on 2026-11-13 (`../dagda/TODO/2026-11-13-three-systems-design.md`, "Ruled") and
Lugh's needs of the two libraries (`2026-11-13-lugh-needs-of-the-stack.md`). Each step is a commit with its tests; each ends green
under ASan with UBSan and under TSan, repeated and shuffled, as `CLAUDE.md` says.

Each statement is **Settled** (the maintainer's, with where) or **Proposed** (for the maintainer).

## Where Lugh is

Everything Lugh persists goes through one interface, `lugh/store/store.h`: named tables with unique indexes; `get`, `find` by
index, `list`; `put` and `remove` conditional on a version (0: must not exist), a removal final and its key never reused. Under
it sits the interim sealed store (`lugh/store/`) and keyring (`lugh/keys/`), frozen and marked for deletion (the maintainer: "Keep the
store until Dagda"). `lugh/test/store_contract_test.cpp` states the interface's contract, and `MemoryStore` is the tests' copy.
State held only in memory today: authorisation codes, PAR URIs and waiting requests, device grants, the `private_key_jwt`
replay cache, DPoP proof `jti`s, revoked access-token `jti`s, opaque-token handles (all `Expiring<T>`), sessions' in-memory
copy, back-channel logouts waiting, and the sign-in back-off (`Backoff`, per node).

## The approach (Proposed)

Swap what sits under the interface before changing anything above it. `DagdaStore` implements `store::Store` over Dagda's
spaces, proved by the same contract test that `MemoryStore` and the sealed store pass; then Lugh's code above runs unchanged on
one node, and what a cluster changes is taken one concern at a time. This keeps P6's and LDAP's 658 tests as the guard
throughout, and puts every cluster behaviour in a step of its own with its own tests.

## Where each table goes

| Table | Space | Placement (Dagda) | Level | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `users`, `groups`, `memberships`, `scope_grants`, `apps`, `app_access`, `policies`, `consents`, `counters` | `lugh/scope` | every node | Replicated | Every node checks scope at the write floor (Settled: stack Decided 6) |
| `credentials`, `factors`, `app_passwords`, `signing_keys`, `sessions`, `grants` (refresh families) | `lugh/secrets` | `capable("lugh")`, configured-member domain until the catalogue | Replicated | Readable only on Lugh nodes, and held on every one of them (Settled: stack 6; design, Lugh's review 1 and 2) |
| `audit` | a node-local log per node | `node_local`, refuse at the bound, retire on export | Persisted | Never dropped, never rolled back (design, Lugh's review 3) |
| codes, PAR, device grants, replay caches | node-local space | `node_local` | Persisted | Single-use on the issuing node (P6 design; stack requirement b) |
| TOTP last step, recovery-code use | `lugh/secrets`, under a per-user lease in one lease space | lease space placed with `lugh/secrets` | Replicated | the maintainer, 2026-11-13; fails closed on a side that cannot reach the lease replicas |
| Sign-in back-off | counter map with forgetting slots | `lugh/scope` or its own every-node space | Persisted | Counted across nodes, forgets after an hour (design, review 4) |
| Published signing keys | register map | every node | Replicated | Lir's verifier (P6 decision 3) |

A record's key in a space is `<table>/<key>`; its value is Lugh's existing record bytes, so every codec stays. Lugh's version
field (Settled: stack Decided 17, ETags) moves into the value: `put` with `expected` is a `Context::Heads::mutate` whose
mutation checks the version in the view it is handed and refuses with `conflict`.

## The merge (Proposed)

One merge for `lugh/scope` and `lugh/secrets`, over the record map, given the common ancestor (Dagda's `Merge::merge(base,
heads)`):

- A key changed on one side only: that side.
- A key changed on both: the higher version, then the higher writing node's id, deterministically. Credentials are versioned,
  so the newer credential wins.
- A removal on either side wins over any edit: tombstones are kept for ever and a removed key is never written again (Settled:
  stack Decided 16; the store contract's "removal is final").
- Unique indexes: two live records with one index value (two `alice`s made in a split) are both kept, and the index marks the
  value ambiguous. Sign-in and LDAP bind by an ambiguous name are refused until an administrator renames one (Settled: stack
  requirement e). Tokens carry the immutable id, so OAuth is unaffected.

## Derived indexes and caches

Every index and the in-memory copy of sessions follow `Heads::watch` and `Heads::changed` (slice 1): a change on any node
reaches every other node's memory as fast as the commit does. Slice 0 measures that latency for Lugh's shape; it replaces the
session refresh's one-minute poll (the maintainer confirmed the minute as built; the notice makes it seconds, without a change of rule).

## What a cluster changes, stated

- **The partition bound** (Settled: stack d): a disabled account or revoked session is honoured on the other side of a split until
  it heals.
- **TOTP and recovery codes fail closed** on a side that cannot reach the lease space's replicas (the maintainer, 2026-11-13).
- **A node newly advertising `lugh`** cannot open `lugh/secrets` until configuration names it a member, until Dagda's catalogue
  (slice 5); sign-in fails on that node until then.
- **Duplicate names** from a split are refused at sign-in until renamed.
